alt: "Lore interlude text: Dragons in Anno Arcana are, on the surface, more or less what you'd expect: large, flying, magical, chimeric reptiles capable of breathing what they believe to be one of the building blocks of nature. Metal, fire, uranium, rubber ducks... whatever their odd minds end up coming up with. Dig a little deeper, though, and you'll find that their spirits are born from the ley lines of their home dimensions. The process is highly unpredictable, to the point that most dragons alive today are actually artificial in origin, like that Earth hybrid you've been following here. These spirits can either merge with a living being or manifest independently. From there, dragons reproduce the old-fashioned way. There is some speculation that their instinct to hoard serves an ecological function, triggering trophic cascades that help keep a nexus healthy. Considering the damage a fully grown dragon can do to a dimension by simply continuing to hoard, this is probably just propaganda from their empires. Despite their penchant for founding kingdoms, dragons are mostly solitary creatures, aside from sharing family groups in a way that may remind Earthlings of polar bears. When they need assistants, servants, or an entire workforce, they generally prefer to make slaves of their own rather than rely on allies. This is why we have ghaals (a short amphibious gremlin creature they made for gathering deep-sea treasures like pearls). They are (relative to many other creatures in the wider multiverse) rare beasts, but the reputation they garnered as empire-destroying monsters still haunts the mind of many an elven monarch or hogwalker merchant, even if their heyday across the multiverse has long since faded. Woe betide the fool who mistakes endangered for extinct. For these creatures are stronger, faster, and older than most."
