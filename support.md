Thank you for reading ***Anno Arcana***! If you'd like to help keep the comic going, here are some ways to support it.


- **Share the comic.** Telling a friend or reposting a page helps more than you'd think.
- **Leave a comment.** Every comic page has a comment section, and comments are always appreciated.
- **Follow along** on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/forscher22.bsky.social).
